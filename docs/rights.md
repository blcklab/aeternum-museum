# Content credits and rights

AETERNUM includes third-party artworks and audio alongside authored world data. No blanket license for the included content is asserted.

- Artwork attribution and source metadata are in `assets/museum/manifest.json`.
- Recording and environmental audio credits are in `assets/audio/soundscapes.json` and the associated asset metadata.
- The two classical recordings carry author-supplied CC BY-NC 4.0 labels pending source verification. Verify the recordings themselves, not only the underlying compositions.
- The two scout models are original test assets from the acceptance lab. The three VRMA animation files were supplied by the author; their redistribution permissions also require verification.

The source's existing rights audit has not been documented as complete. Verify the applicable rights, attribution and use restrictions for every distributed asset before publishing or commercial use. Public GitHub repositories distribute the original files even without Pages. Do not treat a successful technical test or a release-candidate tag as publication clearance.

The review export is intended for inspection until the rights and acceptance gates are satisfied. Preserve the individual credits when distributing cleared assets.
