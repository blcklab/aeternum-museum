# Explore AETERNUM

AETERNUM includes thirteen galleries, an entrance hall and a grand promenade. Explore at your own pace or follow a guided tour from the visitor directory.

## Controls

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| I | Character selection and animation previews |
| M | Visitor directory, map, guided tours and visitor settings |
| N | Neirox soundtrack and environmental audio |
| E | Interact with nearby exhibits and surfaces |
| Esc | Close an active popup |

On touch devices, use the Loader's on-screen controls. If closing a popup releases pointer lock, click the viewport to resume mouse look.

## Characters

First person is the world default. Choose Cyan Museum Scout or Amber Museum Scout in the Character panel, or choose **No character** to return to first person. A previously saved selection takes precedence over the world default. The two scouts share the supplied idle, walk and jump animation files, with distinct preview actions.

## Directory and tours

Choose a gallery and select **Travel to gallery**. Guided tours track confirmed arrivals; they do not move you automatically. Visitor settings provide reading and supported camera preferences. The Loader manages navigation permission: a matching host-approved snapshot can connect automatically, while other worlds may ask you to approve or remember access. The world itself cannot grant permission.

## Artwork and audio

Interact with artwork to inspect it. The Loader can choose smaller artwork textures on mobile; original images remain available for inspection. Use the soundtrack panel to control playback and volume. Audio starts after a browser gesture when required and respects visitor preferences.

See [hosting](hosting.md) for opening the museum from a local server or GitHub repository.
